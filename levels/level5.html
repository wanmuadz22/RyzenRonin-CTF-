The Shogun's Cookie

The dojo remembers who you are. But who decides what it remembers?

When you entered this hall, the dojo handed you a small token that states your rank. Scroll #5 may only be read by the shogun, the highest rank in the land.

The guards never double-check. They simply read whatever token you carry…

Rank check

Your current rank: …

Hint 1

The "token" is a cookie. Cookies are stored in your browser, which means you can read and change them.

Hint 2

DevTools → Application tab → Storage → Cookies (Chrome/Edge), or Storage tab → Cookies (Firefox). Double-click a value to edit it.

Hint 3

Change your rank cookie's value to shogun, then reload. Console alternative: document.cookie = "dojo_rank=shogun"
