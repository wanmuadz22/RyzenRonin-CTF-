The Sealed Gate

The final gate. Sealed shut, guarded by a clearance check you can't even see.

The last scroll lies behind the Sealed Gate. The button to open it has been disabled, and even if you could press it, the gate checks your clearance, which is quietly submitted with the form.

Only those with shogun clearance may pass. You are… not that. Yet.

The Sealed Gate

Hint 1

Two obstacles: a disabled button and a hidden value. Both live in the HTML, and you can edit the live HTML with DevTools.

Hint 2

In the Elements/Inspector tab, find the <button> and delete its disabled attribute (right-click → Edit attribute, or double-click it).

Hint 3

Just above the button is an <input type="hidden">. Double-click its value and change it to shogun. Then click the button.
