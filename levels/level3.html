Where Robots Fear to Tread

Websites tell search-engine robots where not to go. Humans can read those instructions too.

The dojo keeps an archive that no search engine may index. Its keepers asked the crawling robots politely to stay away, and they wrote that request in a public text file that every website traditionally keeps at its root.

Hiding something by telling everyone where it is? Not very ninja.

Note: the "root" of this challenge is the folder containing the dojo's index.html.

Hint 1

The file is part of the "Robots Exclusion Protocol". Its name is robots.txt.

Hint 2

Go back to the dojo's main page and replace index.html in the address bar with robots.txt (or just add robots.txt after the final /).

Hint 3

Look at the Disallow: lines. Visit that path relative to the dojo root.
