The Scroll of Source

What you see on a web page is only what the browser chooses to show you.

The old sensei wrote his lessons on scrolls. The page you're reading is one of them. But sensei was careless. He left a note for himself in the margins, where students were never meant to look.

A true ronin reads the whole scroll, not just the pretty part.

Hint 1

Browsers render HTML, but they don't show everything in it. Some parts are only notes for developers.

Hint 2

Right-click the page and choose View Page Source, or press Ctrl+U (Cmd+Option+U on Mac).

Hint 3

HTML comments look like <!-- like this -->. Search the source for them.
